When am I financially independent?

Not one date — a distribution. This runs thousands of simulated futures entirely in your browser (nothing is sent to a server) and shows how many of them reach your FI target, and by when. Educational purposes only, not financial advice.

Median wealth Worst-to-best tenth band Steady 5% path (what a spreadsheet would say) FI target

How it works: each year, wealth grows by a random return drawn from a Normal distribution (your mean and volatility), plus that year's contribution (contributions grow 3% a year, matching the example series). FI year = the first year wealth reaches your target. Percentiles use the same method as Excel's PERCENTILE.INC. Same seed = same result in this page. Note: seeds here are not comparable to the R engine version — the two use different random number generators — but the distributions match.
